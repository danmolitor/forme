Items discussed

1
Minutes of 7 August and matters arising. Approved without amendment; four of six carried actions are closed. The freight dispute on invoice INV-4102 is now the oldest item on the ledger at 101 days; M. Okonjo has sent the documentation and expects closure by 19 September.
2
Safety. Sixty-two days without a recordable injury. Two near misses in August, both lifting-related at Dock 2, closed with a revised sling inspection routine, which H. Delacroix asked be moved to the daily start-of-shift check; agreed.
3
August operating result. Revenue 11,204 thousand against a plan of 10,800, helped by the second Kestrel panel shipping a week early; gross margin 25.1 percent. The chair noted that the early shipment pulls revenue out of September and asked that the forecast be restated rather than left flattering.
4
Scrap and the Elkhart housings. Twenty-two of sixty bearing housings from Tanaka Precision required rework on the bore, taking scrap to 2.1 percent against a target of 1.6. The supplier has accepted the cost, and first-article inspection now applies to every machined line. The bore gauge recalibration, due in October, will be brought forward.
5
On-time delivery. 92.6 percent against a 95 percent target, fourteen late lines of 189, twelve of them tracing to the housings. Release R-07 is at risk by three days; K. Alvarez to expedite the eight back-ordered brackets at Northmoor's cost.
6
Approval thresholds. The two-signature threshold falls to 10,000 dollars on 1 October; the ERP change will be tested in the sandbox first and plant supervisors briefed at the Tuesday production meeting.
7
Any other business. None. Next meeting Friday 2 October 2026, 09:00.
