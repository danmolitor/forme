# termination-letter

A one-page redundancy letter with a three-row termination-payments table whose gross total is the sum of its rows (24,317.59 in data.json). Change the employee block, dates and payment rows first, keeping the total reconciling. Per the accent policy this document carries no coloured figure or stamp — even the payments keyline is ink, which this template overrides locally from the shared accent subtotal style.

Porting note: the signature pen-room spacer is 9pt here (the prototype uses 22px; font-metric drift between the prototype Georgia and the engine Times left this page 7.5pt over its box, and the pen room is the letter class's content-free slack — the prototypes themselves vary it per letter). The foot's Ref. cell is omitted: the reference already prints in the bottom-left counter line on every page, and the duplicate forced the enclosures onto two lines.

Engine features exercised: @page margin boxes, page counters, fixed-height page fill, data table with an ink subtotal keyline, th scope.
