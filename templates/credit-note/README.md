# credit-note

A credit note issued against an invoice, with a Credit stamp, reason-coded credit lines and a tax-reversal totals stack. Change the against-invoice reference, the credit lines and the effect-on-invoice footer first; the revised balance must equal invoice total minus total credit (data.json mirrors the chain to INV-4417).

Engine features exercised: @page margin boxes with page counters, border-collapse tables, flex shrink-wrapped stamp, accent amount block.
