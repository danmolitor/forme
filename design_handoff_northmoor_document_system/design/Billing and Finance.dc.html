Billing & finance, ten of thirty

Two voices, held to across all thirty: Georgia for titles, prose and every figure that matters, Helvetica at 7px with 0.17em tracking for labels, table heads and anything structural. Titles set at 34px against 7px labels — a 5:1 scale, so hierarchy comes from contrast rather than from boxes and bands.

One accent, oxblood, and it does a different job in each document class. In billing it owns the money: the head rule, the section subtotal keylines, and the amount due, set at 34px over a 2px rule with nothing else near it. Nothing else on the page is coloured. In grayscale the oxblood drops to a dark neutral and the hierarchy survives intact.

US Letter, 816 × 1056 px, 76px margins. Continuation pages appear beside their first page.
